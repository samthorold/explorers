# Viability

The *a priori* lens on the world rules. [World rules](world-rules.md) say what the mechanisms are; [expected properties](expected-properties.md) say what should emerge if the mechanisms are calibrated well. This document sits between them and asks: **given a parameterization, can it produce the expected properties at all — before we ever run the simulation?**

Formally, the [execution model](execution-model.md) defines a discrete dynamical system: the tick loop is a map `state_{t+1} = T(state_t)` whose phase order is fixed. Viability analysis is the study of that map's fixed points — **where in world-parameter space does `T` admit a non-trivial, persistent living state, and where does every trajectory collapse into one of the degenerate configurations?** This is not implementation; it is the mathematics of the map the execution model specifies, and it is the most detailed element of system design that remains implementation-agnostic.

## What this document is, and is not

This document **commits nothing**. It introduces no new mechanism, no new functional form, no new parameter. Every result here is a *consequence* of what the world rules and execution model already commit. Its sole output is the **functional form of the parameter regions that cannot work** — the regions where no amount of search will find life, because the physics forbids it.

This has a direct payoff for [world genesis](world-rules.md). The genesis search is expensive (ensembles of replicate runs); viability analysis is nearly free (arithmetic). Running the gates first **shrinks the search box** by excluding provably-dead parameterizations before any ensemble is spent. The search never goes away — that is its purpose — but it should not waste runs on a region viability can rule out in closed form.

When a region's deadness *cannot* be decided here, that is itself a finding: the system design is under-committed at that point. The fix is to commit the missing detail in system design (world rules / execution model) — never to invent it here.

## How much can be decided a priori

The sharpness of a gate is bounded by how much of the map `T` is pinned. Three tiers:

- **Fully pinned** — the phase order; the kappa flow-allocation rule; the linear nutrient demand `demand = base_nutrient_ratio + spec_coeff · (autotrophy + heterotrophy + mobility)`; the route-agnostic nutrient split; both conservation laws; proportional competition; the reproduction gate. Here the math is exact, symbolic in a few constants.
- **Form pinned, coefficients searched** — convex/superlinear trait maintenance; movement cost linear in distance; the monotone structural death threshold; the exponential efficiency-vs-distance trophic-transfer form (`base_trophic_efficiency · exp(−trophic_distance_decay · d)`, [world rules](world-rules.md), flow 7). Here the math is parametric: real inequalities and comparative statics.
- **Open** — the photosynthesis income magnitude (it now carries a config-only per-tick *cap*, `F·m²`, below — but no committed magnitude); the wear accumulation/repair law; the living-energy stock — no per-unit drain on reserve or on the reproductive allocation is committed, so no closed-form ceiling on living energy exists (the open finding below); and the **minimal-body floor** `S_min` of the energy-death gate — the peak-relative death threshold commits no absolute structure floor, so the gate's `structure_min` has no committed value (the rules support only `S_min → 0`) and the implemented prefilter carries an uncommitted energy anchor, `STRUCTURE_MIN = 1 E`, named but not chosen (the gate below). Here only structural or bounding statements are possible.

A gate is cheap and decidable today exactly when it rests on the first two tiers.

## Dimensionless groups

The committed rules carry four base units — energy `E`, nutrient `N`, length `L`, and the tick `T` — and every gate is a statement about ratios, not magnitudes. Traits are dimensionless; counts are dimensionless. Of the world-parameter fields, `solar_flux_magnitude`, `base_metabolic_rate`, the four trait maintenance costs, `wear_rate`, and `network_maintenance_cost` are energy rates (`E/T`); `reproduction_energy_threshold`, `initial_energy_per_agent`, and `network_creation_cost` are energies; `reproduction_nutrient_threshold` and `initial_nutrient_pool` are nutrient; `base_nutrient_ratio` and `specification_nutrient_coefficient` are `N/E`; `world_extent`, `light_competition_radius`, `nutrient_grid_cell_size`, and the contact, sensing, and dispersal-reach coefficients are lengths (the last three per trait unit); `movement_cost_coefficient` is `1/L`, `body_reach_coefficient` is `L/E^½`; `structure_maintenance_coefficient`, `reserve_mobilisation_rate`, and `network_redistribution_rate` are per-tick rates, `growth_retention_multiplier` is a time; `wear_degradation_steepness` and `repair_decay` are `1/E`. Everything else — efficiencies, exponents, probabilities, trait-space distances, fractions, caps — is already a pure number.

The tick fixes the time scale; `base_metabolic_rate` `B`, `world_extent` `L`, and `reproduction_nutrient_threshold` `N_r` fix the other three (`ε = B·τ` is one tick of base metabolism). The map depends on **49 independent dimensionless groups**: 23 fields that are pure numbers, and 26 ratios —

- energy rates over `B`: `π_F = F/B`, `π_a`, `π_h`, `π_μ`, `π_x` (the four maintenance costs), `π_w` (wear), `π_nm` (network maintenance);
- energy stocks over `ε`: `π_Er` (reproduction threshold), `π_E0` (founder endowment), `π_nc` (connection cost); inverse energies times `ε`: `π_k`, `π_rd`;
- per-tick rates times `τ`: `π_s` (structure maintenance), `π_f` (mobilisation), `π_nr` (redistribution); `π_ret` (retention buffer, in ticks);
- lengths over `L`: `π_r` (light radius), `π_c` (contact), `π_sn` (sensing), `π_dr` (dispersal reach), `π_cell` (nutrient cell); `π_mv = c_mv·L`; `π_body = body_reach·√ε/L`;
- nutrient: `π_N = N_total/N_r`, `π_ρ = ρ_b·ε/N_r`, `π_ρs = ρ_s/ρ_b`.

The genesis search box is 32-dimensional in raw parameters and spans 29 of these groups; its other three axes are the energy, length, and nutrient scales, which act only through ratios to un-searched constants. In the groups, the gates read: extinction `π_F ≤ 1`; energy death `π_N ≥ π_ρ·π_S·(1 + π_ρs·σ_min) + 1`, with the minimal-body floor as its own group `π_S = S_min/ε` (`S_min → 0` under the peak-relative death threshold, so the *committed* floor is `π_N ≥ 1`; the *implemented* prefilter sets `S_min = STRUCTURE_MIN = 1 E`, so its `π_S = 1/ε` moves with `B` — see *Unit anchors* below and the gate); the producer-invasion ratio `ρ = π_F/b_P` and consumer-invasion number `I = χ_C·γ·e·ĥ_C·π_F/(b_P·b_C)` of the permanence reading; the lockup repeller `Λ = ĥ_C·ι·(π_N/ν̂)·min(χ_C·γ·e_C, q/θ_C)/b_C`; and the sustained-count ceiling `N̄_max = π_F·m²`, `m = ⌊√2/π_r⌋ + 1`, where `b_X = B_X/B` is a body's maintenance in base-metabolisms and `ĥ_C` the consumer's per-target drain in the same units. The full unit table, the derivation, and the three places the committed rules break dimensional homogeneity (the implicit unit constants in the trait-to-flow conversions, `use_wear_rate`, and the prefilter's `STRUCTURE_MIN`) are in [`440-dimensionless-groups.md`](../research/440-dimensionless-groups.md). The groups are a statement of what the rules *can* read, not of what decides outcomes over the box: measured on the #509 sweep, `ρ`, `I`, `Λ` and `κ_C` together explain ~5 % of a config's live and lockup fractions where the raw coordinates explain ~35–40 %, almost all of it through `light_competition_radius` — the coordinate the count ceiling's `m²` carries and the permanence reading does not ([`462-pi-space-explanatory-check.md`](../research/462-pi-space-explanatory-check.md)).

**Unit anchors.** Five trait-to-flow conversions carry a unit constant that pins the dimensionless trait unit to a base unit: `u_M = 1 L/T` (mobility → distance moved per tick), `u_D = 1 L` (dispersal → placement-kernel `σ`), `u_A = 1 N/T` (autotrophy → nutrient uptake demand), `u_H = 1 E/T` (heterotrophy → structure drained per target per tick), `u_R = 1 E/T` (kappa → base repair per functional trait). They are named in [`world-rules`](world-rules.md) (*Unit anchors*) and in code as `explorers_sim::units`; every value is `1.0` in today's units, and naming them changed no trajectory. They matter here because the groups above are dimensionless in raw parameters *only through them*: the consumer's drain `ĥ_C = h_C·u_H/B` in `I` and `Λ` is a pure number because `u_H` carries the `E/T`, and the three scale axes of the search box (`B`, `L`, `base_nutrient_ratio`) act through the ratios `u_M·τ/L`, `u_D/L`, `u_A·τ/N_r`, `u_H·τ/ε`, `u_R·τ/ε`. Whether to expose any anchor as a parameter is deferred (#459); until then a gate written in the groups should be read with the anchors at `1`. Use-dependent wear carries a second family of three (#460): `u_WA = 1 E/E` (per energy captured), `u_WH = 1 E/E` (per energy drained), `u_WM = 1 E/L` (per distance moved), named in `explorers_sim::units` as `USE_WEAR_PER_*`. They make the one-coefficient `use_wear_rate` readable as a dimensionless number for the two energy couplings while `u_WM` alone carries the `E/L` of the mobility coupling; the field is `0` on the search baseline, so no gate above depends on it, and its dimensional fix (split the coefficient, or convert distance moved to its energy cost first) is deferred to the designer. A third anchor lives not in the stepper but in the search: the energy-death prefilter's `STRUCTURE_MIN = 1 E` (`explorers_search::prefilter::STRUCTURE_MIN`), the value it substitutes for the gate's symbolic `S_min`. Unlike the trait-to-flow anchors it pins no trait unit — it is an energy the rules never committed (Open tier, above), so `π_S = STRUCTURE_MIN/ε = 1/ε` and the implemented gate is not scale-free: a config with the same physics at a different `base_metabolic_rate` gets a different verdict. Its magnitude is harmless today (`≤ ρ_b·1 E` against `N_r`, at most a third of the floor across the search box, with the baseline pool four orders above it; every committed atlas live cell clears it, pinned by `tests/prefilter_atlas.rs`), but whether to drop the term (`π_N ≥ 1`) or commit a minimal-body floor in system design is deferred to the designer (#461).

## Organising principle: the failure modes as gates

The degenerate configurations in [expected properties](expected-properties.md) are the natural index. Each is a way the map `T` fails to hold a living fixed point, and they split by how amenable they are to a priori analysis:

- **Existence failures — cheaply provable dead-zones.** These ask "does a living fixed point exist at all?" — exactly what static analysis answers. *Extinction* and *energy death* are gated below. *Population explosion* in **count** is the existence side's one affirmative statement so far: the sustained-count ceiling below shows the long-run mean population is bounded by `π_F·m²` under the committed rules — a ceiling every living state respects, not a region ruled out — while explosion in *stored energy* is an open finding (the rules commit no drain on the reproductive allocation, so no energy ceiling exists).
- **Dynamics failures — out of cheap a priori reach (for now).** *Monoculture* vs. coexistence, *frozen dynamics* vs. oscillation, *generalist dominance*, and *nutrient lockup* are not about whether a fixed point exists but about its *stability and multiplicity* — bifurcation and stability territory. (Nutrient lockup shares the nutrient-floor lineage of the energy-death gate but is realized dynamically, through decomposer reach and turnover rather than a static `N_total` inequality; it is the one mode of this group now *partially* reached — a conditional decomposer-return floor and a closed-form flux-balance characterisation, both below.) (Generalist dominance was once filed as a candidate existence gate; the analysis below shows its prevention rests on structural fragility and functional incompatibility — a mortality-and-morphology force, not a static income/cost inequality — so it belongs here.) The rest remain the domain of the search until the affirmative theory is built out; nutrient lockup is where its first piece has been placed, and the *boundary* of the frozen↔oscillation coordinate — whether a producer↔consumer pair and a heterotroph on the carcass pile can hold off extinction and lockup at all — now carries a closed-form **permanence** characterisation of the mean-field reduction (below). (Two of these — *frozen↔oscillation* and *monoculture↔coexistence* — now carry a closed-form *distance-to-bifurcation* reading wired into the search as a **descriptor plus cross-check**, not a gate; see [genesis search, *Predicted bifurcation coordinates and the cross-check*](genesis-search.md#predicted-bifurcation-coordinates-and-the-cross-check). These readings are conductance-aware: by reserve conservation the mobilised growth flow equals net income at any reserve fixed point, so the branching margin and the fixed-point location are invariant to the [reserve mobilisation rate](world-rules.md) `f`, while the oscillation reading carries reserve as an explicit slow state so its Hopf boundary tracks `f`.) Because the median-over-seeds selection signal is itself high-variance near this monoculture↔coexistence bifurcation, the playable-world projection additionally reads a reported per-seed `coexistence_fraction` and prefers a cell most of whose ensemble coexists — so a lucky-draw straddler is not certified as the recipe (see [genesis search, *The recipe is a projection*](genesis-search.md#the-recipe-is-a-projection-of-the-atlas)). This is a selection/characterisation note, not a gate: the straddler cell is still mapped, and the fraction never enters fitness or binning.

> **Direction of travel.** This document began as a set of *negative gates* — proofs that a parameterization does **not** fail in a given way — and the gates remain the negative-space sketch of an *affirmative* theory: constructive conditions under which a property **will** appear. That theory now has two results, held at two different authorities, and the distinction matters. Its first **gate-grade** result is the **sustained-count ceiling** (`N̄_max = π_F·m²`): a closed-form bound every living state obeys, proven from the committed energy budget alone and unviolated on every genesis run checked. Its first **constructive** result is the **permanence of the mean-field reduction** (below): a closed-form condition — the producer-invasion ratio `ρ`, the consumer-invasion number `I`, and the lockup repeller `Λ` — under which the extinction and lockup boundaries of the averaged system *repel*, so that the living state persists (cycling or not) rather than merely exists. Permanence is a **characterisation, not a gate**: it holds on the reduction, it is contradicted in the individual-based model on named cells, and it is wired into nothing. Between them sits the older affirmative work on **nutrient lockup** — the conditional decomposer-return floor and the flux-balance characterisation (`C* = death flux ÷ decomposition rate`) — affirmative where the committed fluxes permit, and honestly short of a cheap gate where decomposer mass is emergent. The lesson of the permanence result is the same one: the reduction's *negative* direction is safe and its *positive* direction promises nothing per seed.

## Gate — extinction (flux floor)

*Form-free.* Producer income is bounded by the solar flux in its light-competition neighbourhood; an isolated producer receives at most the full flux magnitude `F`. Metabolism charges at least the base rate `B` every tick (flow 8). So the per-tick net of any producer is at most `F − B`.

> **If `F ≤ B`, no producer is ever net-positive — no energy enters the living system — extinction, for every other parameter and every functional form.**

This is the weakest necessary condition for life — clearing it is far from sufficient — but failing it is a guaranteed kill, decidable from two numbers.

## Bound — sustained population (solar ceiling)

*Form-free on the income side; fully pinned on the drain side.* The extinction gate's two ingredients — solar flux is the sole external energy source, and metabolism charges at least `B` every tick — combined with the geometry of light competition, give the first **affirmative** statement on the existence side. It is not a region where life is impossible; it is a ceiling that every living state must respect, however the searched coefficients fall.

**Per-tick solar income is capped.** A producer's light share is `own_weight / Σ weights of producers within r` (flow 1), so the shares of any set of producers that are all mutually within `r` sum to at most 1, and that set draws at most `F` in a tick. Tile the torus into `m × m` square cells with `m = ⌊√2·L/r⌋ + 1`: each cell's side is `< r/√2`, so every producer in a cell is within `r` of every other (toroidal distance never exceeds Euclidean), and each cell contributes at most `F`. Hence total photosynthetic income in any tick obeys

```
P(t)  ≤  F · min(N_P(t), m²)          m = ⌊√2·L/r⌋ + 1  =  ⌊√2/π_r⌋ + 1
```

with `N_P(t)` the embodied producers (`structure > 0`, positive autotrophy). The config-only form is `P_max = F·m²`, or `P̂_max = π_F·m²` in base-metabolisms per tick. This is the bounding statement the Open tier can make about the photosynthesis income magnitude: net primary production per tick is at most `F·m² − B·N_P`. (It requires `r > 0`; and `m²` is a tiling constant, not a packing constant, so the geometric branch is not tight.)

**Total energy is dissipative per survivor.** Let `E_tot` be living energy (reserve + reproductive allocation + structure) plus the carcass pool, and `N_s(t)` the agents alive at the start of tick `t` that are still alive at its end. Conservation gives `E_tot(t+1) = E_tot(t) + P(t) − D(t)`, the endowment being booked once at genesis. Every survivor was charged its full metabolic cost `≥ B` (flow 8), paid it, and ended the tick with reserve still positive — so that `B` is a net loss to `E_tot` — and every other dissipation is non-negative. So `D(t) ≥ B·N_s(t)` and

```
E_tot(t+1)  ≤  E_tot(t) + P(t) − B · N_s(t)
```

**The sustained-count ceiling.** Summing over `T` ticks, `B·Σ_{t<T} N_s(t) ≤ E_tot(0) + T·P_max`, so the long-run mean number of tick-survivors obeys

> `(1/T)·Σ_{t<T} N_s(t)  ≤  N̄_max + E_tot(0)/(B·T)`,   **`N̄_max = F·m²/B = π_F·m²`**.

The transient is `t_0 = E_tot(0)/(B·ε)`: past it the mean survivor count is within `ε` of `N̄_max` (in the groups, `t_0/τ = Ê_tot(0)/ε̂` — the founder endowment in ticks of base metabolism, over the tolerance). On the default world (`r > L`, so `m = 1`) the ceiling is simply `π_F`; on the viable baseline (`L = 100`, `r = 8`) `m = 18`.

The bound rests on: the sole solar source and the once-booked endowment; proportional light share weighted by structure; the toroidal, strict `< r` neighbourhood; the fixed phase order, with the population at a tick's start equal to the previous tick's survivors; metabolism `≥ B` charged unconditionally on the non-negative trait domain the world rules assume; every other flow either internal to `E_tot` or to heat; and the energy conservation law. Reproduction, growth, and death enter only as dissipations — which is why the bound needs no "minimal viable body": the death threshold is peak-relative, so no such body exists in the committed rules, and the count goes through metabolism instead.

**Authority boundary.**

- *Time-averaged, not instantaneous.* A hoarded reproductive allocation can be discharged into a Poisson-sized brood that lives a few ticks, so `N(t)` can spike past `N̄_max` transiently. Only the mean is bounded.
- *Necessary, not predictive.* `N̄_max` counts agents paying only `B`; every real agent also pays trait and structure maintenance, so an ecology sits well below it. It is not a gate — it rules out no region before a run — and it is not wired into the prefilter. Its energy form, `N̄_max·ε = F·m²·τ` (the stock a population at the ceiling holds to pay one tick of base metabolism), is the history-free reference the rollout's [energy-death](expected-properties.md#energy-death) read compares the living stock against — a read on a trajectory, not a prefilter — promoted after the zero-false-positive check ([508](../research/508-energy-death-sustainable.md)).
- *Tightness.* Over 2256 genesis runs at the settled horizon `T = 2000` — the 82 atlas live cells and 200 low-discrepancy points of the search box, 8 seeds each, on the stepper with the founder floor; 67 runs on 16 dense configs stopped by the 300 s wall-clock guard and excluded, leaving 2189 checked — no run violates either inequality. The income cap is tight on the `N_P` branch and is reached exactly (observed/bound median 1.000, lower quartile 0.83, maximum 1.000 to the ledger's f32; 1361 of 2189 runs sit at `≥ 0.999`): a settled community is sparse, and a producer with no competitor within `r` takes the full flux. The bound is per tick, so the runs the guard stopped early would count too — none of their ticks exceeds it either. The `m²` branch never binds, since peak populations exceed `m²` in only 593 runs and never by enough for the tiling to be the tighter cap. The count bound holds with two orders of magnitude to spare: the whole-run mean of `N_s` over `N̄_max` has median 0.0032 and maximum 0.054, and the realised form `B·ΣN_s / (E_tot(0) − E_tot(T) + ΣP)` has median 0.12 and maximum 0.76 — survivors' base charge is about 12 % of the energy that passes through a system, the rest going to trait and structure maintenance, movement, transfer loss, and the stocks. The 500-tick figures this replaces (income cap median 0.93; count bound median 0.0041, maximum 0.067) are in [`433-energy-bound.md`](../research/433-energy-bound.md); the settled-horizon re-check is [`509-viability-at-settled-horizon.md`](../research/509-viability-at-settled-horizon.md).

This is the *count* half of the [population explosion](expected-properties.md#population-explosion) failure mode: a long-run mean population above `π_F·m²` is energy-impossible. The evaluator's detector is something else — a fixed count ceiling that serves as a runtime performance guard — and this bound does not touch it.

## Open finding — living energy has no ceiling under the committed rules

The companion statement — `E_living(t) ≤ max(E_living(0), E_max)` with `E_max` closed-form in the committed parameters — does **not** hold. The bound above drains `B` *per survivor*, not per unit of energy. A stock bound needs either a per-agent energy ceiling or a drain proportional to the stock, and the committed rules provide neither for reserve or the reproductive allocation: the only per-unit charge is `structure_maintenance_coefficient` on structure (flow 8).

**The obstructing rule is flow 9's reproductive allocation.** It is filled every tick by the `(1 − kappa)` share of mobilised surplus, is charged nothing to hold, has no cap, and is drawn down only by a reproductive event that the rules never force — reproduction needs an asexual roll that never succeeds at propensity 0, or a compatible mate within reach (flow 4). A lone producer with `kappa < 1`, `asexual_propensity = 0`, and no mate therefore gains a fixed positive amount of living energy every tick, forever: `E_living → ∞` while `N = 1`. This survives every point of the search box (the mean propensity may be 0, a lineage's propensity can mutate to 0, mates can be out of reach at any time), and it is the median behaviour rather than a corner: in the same 2256 runs at `T = 2000` the allocation holds 63 % of living energy at peak in the median run (upper quartile 88 %; more than half in 1247 of 2059 survivors), and in 979 of 2059 survivors living energy is still rising at the settled horizon — the hoard has grown since the 500-tick reading (36 %, 815 of 2180), as an untaxed stock must.

**The commitment that would restore the bound — held in reserve, not made.** A per-unit holding cost `h > 0` per tick on reserve and on the reproductive allocation — the shape of the committed `c_s · structure` term of flow 8, extended to the other two living stocks — makes living energy dissipative in itself. With `h' = min(h, c_s)`, in any world whose carcass pool cannot feed back into living stock, `E_living(t) ≤ max(E_living(0), P_max/h')` for all `t` (`t_0 = 0`) — in the groups, `Ê_living ≤ max(π_E0·N_0, π_F·m²/(h'·τ))` — and the count bound sharpens from a time average to an instantaneous `N_s(t) ≤ (E_max + P_max)/B`. With a reachable carcass pool the carcass-to-living inflow stays a separate term, bounded only by the same achievable-reach / carcass-richness commitment the lockup gate declines to spend. (A cap on the allocation with overflow dissipated, or a forced discharge at the cap, would bound the allocation alone and leave unallocated reserve untaxed.) The design takes the same posture here as toward the interaction term and the reach bound: the holding cost is promoted only if genesis search finds hoarding that a viability argument needs to exclude, never to flatter a proof.

Three further gaps, recorded for completeness: the carcass pool is untaxed and never decays, so `E_tot` is unbounded even with the allocation closed — the energy-side twin of nutrient lockup; a negative founder trait under an odd maintenance exponent would turn flow 8's per-trait term into an income, outside the non-negative trait domain the world rules assume — closed since founder seeding floors every trait at zero exactly as mutation does; and `light_competition_radius = 0` removes the geometric cap (`m` is undefined; every producer takes the full `F`) — a legal-parameter corner the search box excludes with `r ≥ 1`.

The proofs, the property tests that hold them falsifiable against the stepper, and the 2256-run check are in [`433-energy-bound.md`](../research/433-energy-bound.md).

## Gate — energy death (nutrient floor)

*Fully pinned.* Nutrient is conserved. A living agent holds free nutrient, a reproductive earmark, and nutrient bound in structure (`structure · demand`). For a lineage to persist, at least one agent must be simultaneously **embodied** (`structure > 0`, required even to photosynthesise — light share is weighted by structure, flow 1) and **reproduction-ready** (earmark `≥ N_repro_threshold`, the reproduction nutrient gate). Both draw on the conserved total. Hence a necessary condition:

```
N_total  ≥  structure_min · (base_nutrient_ratio + spec_coeff · σ_min)   +   N_repro_threshold
              └──── nutrient bound in one minimal viable body ────┘            └─ earmark to clear the gate ─┘
```

where `σ_min` is the specification sum of a minimal viable agent.

In the [dimensionless groups](#dimensionless-groups) this is `π_N ≥ π_ρ · π_S · (1 + π_ρs · σ_min) + 1`, with the minimal-body floor as its own group `π_S = S_min / ε`. **`S_min` is uncommitted.** The committed death threshold is peak-relative (`fragility × peak_structure`, [world rules](world-rules.md)) and supplies no absolute floor, so no minimal viable body exists under the rules ([`433-energy-bound.md`](../research/433-energy-bound.md), B1) and the only value they support is `S_min → 0` — the floor `N_total ≥ N_r`, `π_N ≥ 1`. The implemented prefilter (`explorers_search::prefilter::fails_energy_death_gate`) instead fills the symbol with a named anchor `STRUCTURE_MIN = 1 E` and `σ_min = 0`, so its floor is `N_total ≥ 1 E · base_nutrient_ratio + N_r` and, because `π_S = 1/ε`, the gate it runs moves with `base_metabolic_rate` for no physical reason. This places the floor in the [Open tier](#how-much-can-be-decided-a-priori) alongside the living-energy ceiling: the anchor is visible and citable, its value is today's effective value, and no atlas live cell is mis-gated by it (`tests/prefilter_atlas.rs`); whether to drop the term or commit a floor is a design decision, not one the prefilter makes (#461; [`440-dimensionless-groups.md`](../research/440-dimensionless-groups.md), smell S3).

> **If `N_total` is below this, no agent can be both embodied and able to reproduce — no reproduction, no persistence — regardless of solar flux. Energy abundance cannot rescue a nutrient-starved world.**

The gate is deliberately conservative: it ignores nutrient locked in carcasses in transit (death flux ÷ decomposition rate — given closed form by the flux-balance characterisation below) and the available pool that ongoing uptake needs, both of which only *raise* the real floor. So failing the closed form is sufficient for deadness; clearing it is necessary, not sufficient. This is the analytic statement of the [energy death](expected-properties.md#energy-death) failure mode and the [no-passive-decay](world-rules.md#no-passive-decay) consequence.

The carcass-locked nutrient this static gate deliberately sets aside is exactly what the [nutrient lockup](expected-properties.md#nutrient-lockup) failure mode tracks dynamically: a world can clear `N_total` here yet still strand its nutrient in an unconsumed dead pool when decomposers are absent, out of reach, or non-viable, dragging the *available* pool below the floor over time. So nutrient lockup is a **dynamics** failure — turnover- and reach-dependent — not decidable from `N_total` alone, and it sits with the dynamics modes rather than this existence gate. It nonetheless yields to affirmative analysis as far as the committed fluxes allow: a conditional decomposer-return floor and the flux-balance characterisation that follow this gate.

## Resolved finding — nutrient lockup has no clean existence gate (decomposer-return floor)

Nutrient lockup is the dead-pool sibling of energy death ([expected properties](expected-properties.md#nutrient-lockup)): conserved nutrient silts into carcasses faster than living decomposers return it, dragging the *available* pool below the reproduction floor even when `N_total` clears the static energy-death gate above. The tempting move is a closed-form existence gate paralleling extinction's `F ≤ B` — a **decomposer-return floor**: if no decomposer strategy can net-clear metabolism on carcass return, the dead pool never drains and lockup is guaranteed for every seed. On analysis that gate exists only in a degenerate corner and is otherwise blocked by under-commitment — the same shape as the generalist-dominance finding below.

Decomposition is consumption of a carcass (CONTEXT.md, *Decomposition*), so it runs through the committed trophic transfer: a decomposer retains at most `base_trophic_efficiency · exp(−trophic_distance_decay · d)` ([world rules](world-rules.md), flow 7) of a carcass's content, `d` the trait-space distance from the decomposer to the carcass's preserved trait vector. Against this it pays metabolism every tick — `base_metabolic_rate` plus superlinear trait maintenance of its own body (flow 8). So lockup is *avoided* only if some decomposer strategy clears

```
base_trophic_efficiency · (reachable carcass energy per tick)  ≥  base_metabolic_rate + maintenance(decomposer body)
              └──── best-case carcass return ────┘                    └──── decomposition cost ────┘
```

**Why this is not a clean gate — both sides are endogenous.** A carcass holds exactly the structure-bound energy and nutrient of the agent that died; its richness is set by the world's realized energy economy, not by any committed parameter. The decomposer's cost scales with the body it grows, also endogenous. And return *scales with carcass richness* while cost is roughly fixed per tick, so for a rich enough, in-reach carcass decomposition is eventually net-positive — there is no pure-parameter ceiling that universally forbids it. The only **genuinely a priori, universal kills** are the degenerate corners: `base_trophic_efficiency = 0` (no carcass content is ever recoverable) or decomposer reach driven to zero (no carcass is ever contacted). In either, the per-unit-carcass clearance rate is identically zero, the dead pool is a one-way sink, and lockup is certain for every seed. These are real but rarely bite.

**Reserve remedy.** Between the degenerate kills and a healthy cycle, decomposer viability is *turnover- and reach-dependent* — a dynamics property, not a static inequality. A clean closed-form lockup gate becomes available only if the design later commits a bound on **achievable carcass richness** or **decomposer reach** — the two endogenous terms above — a knob it does not currently spend. The design holds this in reserve, to be promoted only if genesis search finds worlds locking up where the committed parameters say a decomposer should be viable — the same contingency posture the generalist-dominance finding below takes toward a cross-trait interaction term. Until then the prevention is real but dynamic, and the matching gate stays out of reach.

## Characterisation — nutrient lockup as a flux balance

Where the finding above shows lockup has no cheap *gate*, the conserved nutrient cycle does admit an affirmative *characterisation* — a closed-form stationary condition derived entirely from committed fluxes. It is **not** a search-box gate: it cannot rule out a region before a run, because it depends on emergent decomposer mass (see the authority note below). Its value is to sharpen the energy-death caveat from a hand-wave into a stated condition, and to give the [validation triad](#place-in-the-validation-triad) its first falsifiable lockup prediction.

Conserved nutrient partitions into three compartments — the **available pool** `A` (the substrate grid), **living-bound** `L` (free store + reproductive earmark + structure-bound), and **carcass-locked** `C` (the dead pool) — with `A + L + C = N_total`, the closed nutrient ledger balanced every tick. The committed phases move nutrient between them: uptake `A → L`, death `L → C` carrying the agent's exact nutrient, and decomposition `C → A` (the trophic drain run over carcass targets). At stationarity the dead-pool balance `dC/dt = 0` fixes the carcass-locked fraction in closed form:

```
C* = death_flux / decomp_turnover
```

— precisely the *"death flux ÷ decomposition rate"* the energy-death gate names but sets aside — where `decomp_turnover` is the per-unit-carcass clearance rate = (decomposer mass) · (the committed `base_trophic_efficiency · exp(−trophic_distance_decay · d)` kernel at the decomposer-on-carcass distance) · (in-reach geometry). The lockup condition is then **the energy-death floor with `N_total` replaced by the *available* budget**: lockup occurs when stranded `C*` pulls the residual pool `A* = N_total − L_min − C*` below the reproduction floor of the energy-death gate. This is the dynamical generalisation that gate says it "cannot yet reach," and it is reachable precisely because every flux above is already a committed rule.

**Authority boundary.** `C*` contains `decomposer mass`, which is *emergent and per-seed*: a decomposer is a behavioural role read from θ, confirmed across seed ensembles but sporadic per-seed, never guaranteed on a single run ([expected properties](expected-properties.md#nutrient-lockup); CONTEXT.md, *Decomposition*). So the characterisation states whether the *average* world strands its nutrient; it can never say *which seeds* nucleate a decomposer guild. That is the same existence-vs-distributional boundary the gates already respect — and the reason `C*` is a characterisation, not a gate.

**Cross-check.** On `scenarios/example9_detrital_pathway.json` the producer ring's carcass rain falls outside any decomposer's reach, so `decomp_turnover → 0` for the field and `C* →` the full death flux: the characterisation predicts **`nutrient_lockup`**, which is the observed verdict the evaluator reports (8/8). This is the cheapest place to hold the affirmative theory falsifiable — agreement raises confidence; a healthy run against an `A`-starving prediction would localise the decomposition-reach geometry as the faulty term.

## Resolved finding — partial closure is the faithful equilibrium, and the committed reach form is not a gate

A standing dead pool is not a defect to be driven to zero — it is the faithful equilibrium of a conserved nutrient stock. Because nutrient is conserved and death is continuous, the carcass-locked compartment `C` is never empty at any non-degenerate fixed point: every tick death refills it and decomposition drains it, and `C* = death_flux / decomp_turnover` is the balance the two strike. `C* → 0` would require either no death or infinite turnover; neither is reachable, and a real ecosystem likewise always carries standing dead organic matter in transit ([nutrient cycling](../ecology/nutrient-cycling.md) — soil organic matter outweighs living biomass and the atmosphere combined). The single-nutrient model has no biologically-produced *refractory* pool that could strand nutrient permanently — substrate-quality recalcitrance (lignin, C:N immobilisation) is a multi-currency phenomenon the model deliberately omits — and the only permanently-inert pool, `unavailable`, is geological and constant. So the question the conserved cycle poses is not "is `C` small?" but **"does the flux balance leave `A*` above the reproduction floor, with `C` bounded?"** — the energy-death floor read on the *available* budget, exactly as the characterisation above states.

`C` is bounded precisely when `decomp_turnover > 0` across the carcass-fall. Where a decomposer persistently contacts the dying, the reached pool cycles at a finite `C*`; where carcass-fall lands outside every decomposer's reach — the un-reached arcs of a ring blanketed by too few point-decomposers — `decomp_turnover → 0` locally and `C` accumulates there. This *geometric* reach limit, not chemical recalcitrance, is the only residual dead pool the model can offer, and whether it bounds `C` overall on a long horizon is a stability question this lens cannot settle a priori (it turns on emergent guild placement and growth) — genesis search, not a knob, is its instrument.

This is why committing nothing leaves the gate status unchanged. The body-extent reach term — `consumption_reach = effective_heterotrophy · (contact_range_coefficient + body_reach_coefficient · √structure)`, a committed world rule ([world rules](world-rules.md), flow 3) — pins the *shape* of reach but not a *bound on its magnitude*: the coefficient is searched and `structure` is emergent, so achievable reach is per-seed and emergent just as decomposer mass is. `decomp_turnover` therefore keeps its emergent factors, the authority boundary holds, and `C*` stays a **characterisation, not a gate**. The clean lockup gate the decomposer-return floor reaches for still requires a *bound* on achievable reach or carcass richness — the knob the design does not spend (above).

The tempting shortcut — pin `body_reach_coefficient > 0` as a world default because a growing mycelium would then blanket the ring and shrink `C` — is rejected on the discipline that keeps every reserve lever unspent: it would choose a parameter to flatter a scenario, not because the ecology fixes its value. The faithful stance is that the reach *form* is committed, its *magnitude* is for genesis to map, and the living/dead physics asymmetry that would widen the detrital niche stays in reserve ([trait space](trait-space.md)) until genesis finds lockup where a decomposer should be viable — a contingency example13 does not trip.

**Empirical confirmation — the loop closes in the conserved-stock sense (#390, extends #136).** `scenarios/example13_closed_web.json` seeds a lean sessile facultative-detritivore guild on `example12`'s viable differentiated ring, within reach of its carcass rain. Across 8 seeds the guild persists as a reproducing lineage (`failure=none` 8/8, fitness 0.686 ≥ example12's 0.667, `trophic_balance_score` 1.0 — a producer-led pyramid, not inverted), and the brown loop carries flux: carcass-locked nutrient sits ~40% below the no-decomposer baseline while `A*` stays above the reproduction floor. By the conserved-stock criterion this **is** closure — the standing `C*` is the faithful equilibrium, not a shortfall. Its residual gap is the geometric one (`body_reach_coefficient = 0`, so the point-decomposers cannot extend reach to the un-reached arcs): the dormant magnitude of a committed form, left for search to explore, not a missing mechanism.

## Characterisation — permanence of the mean-field reduction

The gates above ask whether a living state *exists*; the lockup characterisation asks where it *sits*. Permanence asks the affirmative question in between: does the living state **persist** — is the extinction boundary a *repeller*, so that every trajectory starting inside stays bounded away from it, cycling or not? For the mean-field reduction of the committed rules the answer is a closed form in the dimensionless groups. It is the first constructive result of the affirmative theory, and it is a **characterisation, not a gate** — for reasons the authority boundary and the failure cells below make concrete.

**The reduction.** Average the committed phases over a well-mixed world with continuous biomass: producer structure `P` and consumer structure `C_E` (the frozen↔oscillation coordinate's two compartments), coupled to the three nutrient compartments of the flux-balance characterisation — available `A`, living `L`, carcass-locked `C_N`, with `A + L + C_N = N_total`. The producer's growth diagonal is photosynthesis less maintenance under the density-dependent light share; the trophic term is the binary-reach drain run over living targets, retained at the committed kernel `e = base_trophic_efficiency · exp(−trophic_distance_decay · d)`; growth is Liebig-limited by uptake `min(α_P·P, A)`; death strands nutrient in `C_N`; and the same drain run over carcasses returns it. Every symbol is a committed parameter or a named lumping of a committed flow (`b_X = B_X/B` a body's maintenance in base-metabolisms; `κ`, `γ`, `θ`, `α`, `h` the cluster's kappa, growth efficiency, stoichiometric demand, autotrophy, and effective heterotrophy; `ĥ_C` the consumer's per-target drain in base-metabolisms; `ι` the fraction of the carcass pile within a consumer's feeding reach; `ν` nutrient per carcass, `ν̂ = ν/N_r`; `q` carcass richness; `μ_P` producer per-tick mortality).

**The condition.** In the groups:

```
ρ  =  π_F / b_P                                              > 1     producer invasion — the producer face has a positive equilibrium
I  =  χ_C · γ · e · ĥ_C · π_F / (b_P · b_C)                  > 1     consumer invasion — the consumer invades the producer standing crop
Λ  =  ĥ_C · ι · (π_N / ν̂) · min(χ_C·γ·e_C, q/θ_C) / b_C      > 1     lockup repeller — a heterotroph invades the lockup corner from the carcass pile
```

- On the two-compartment energy map alone, and under admissible rates (the consumer cannot lose more than its whole body in a tick; the producer's low-density growth factor is at most 2, the discrete logistic's stable range) in the unsaturated regime (total consumer demand below the standing crop — the drain cap never binds), the extinction boundary `{P = 0} ∪ {C_E = 0}` is a repeller **iff `ρ > 1` and `I > 1`**. `ρ > 1` is the extinction gate `π_F > 1` sharpened by the searched maintenance groups (`b_P = 1` is the gate's corner). The oscillating regime lies strictly inside the permanent region: the Hopf line `I = (1 + m)/m`, with `m` the consumer's per-tick maintenance as a fraction of its body, sits above `I = 1`, so permanence admits the cycle, as it must — it constrains the boundary, never the interior attractor.
- On the coupled energy × nutrient reduction, the **dead set is a line of fixed points, not a point**: every state with `P = C_E = 0` is stationary (no passive decay), parameterised by how the nutrient splits between `A` and `C_N`, from the **virgin end** (`A = N_total`) to the **lockup corner** (`C_N = N_total`). The producer-only face flows *into* the lockup corner — every death strands nutrient, nothing returns it — and the consumer-only face flows back toward the virgin end — heterotrophs eat the pile, excrete it into `A`, then starve. The lockup boundary is therefore a **heteroclinic cycle** along the dead line. **Producers cannot invade the lockup corner at any solar flux**: uptake is `min(α_P·P, A)` and `A = 0` there, so the Liebig minimum closes growth at zero. Only a heterotroph feeding on the pile can, and `Λ > 1` is exactly the condition that it does — the carcass-pile twin of `I`, with the producer standing crop `π_F/b_P` replaced by the carcass standing pile `π_N/ν̂` and the kernel acquiring a Liebig branch in carcass richness. Living nutrient `L` then persists when the producer invades the virgin pool (`min(r_P, α_P/θ_P) > μ_P`, with `r_P` the producer's low-density net growth), `Λ > 1`, and the cycle's escape rates outweigh its approach rates (the classical heteroclinic-cycle product criterion). `I` does not appear: on the coupled reduction the consumer's escape route is the pile, not the crop.

Both statements are proven by an average Lyapunov function on the boundary ω-limit set and agree with brute-force boundary-escape numerics on every scored cell of a 25×25 sweep of each reduction.

**Authority boundary.** The condition is a statement about a deterministic, well-mixed, continuous-biomass map, and about nothing else.

- *No demographic noise.* Permanence bounds the trough of the interior attractor away from zero, but says nothing about how far; on the growing cycle above the Hopf line the trough shrinks without bound, and a finite population at a trough of a few bodies dies by chance. Mean-field permanence is therefore **necessary, not sufficient, for individual-based persistence** — the same conservative asymmetry the existence gates carry: *reduction not permanent ⇒ the expected trajectory goes extinct; reduction permanent ⇒ nothing is promised per seed.* The safe direction is the negative one.
- *No space.* The trophic term is an integral over an in-reach indicator the mean field sets to 1, and `ι` is a reach fraction it cannot compute. The **concrete non-claim**: the reduction reads `scenarios/example10_predator_prey_hopf.json` as permanent and oscillating (`ρ = 71`, `I = 23`), and the committed file's consumers go extinct by tick 2 because their reach never meets the sparse standing crop. The gap between "permanent here" and "dead there" localises to the in-reach geometry — a diagnostic, not a defect in the condition.
- *No per-seed or distributional claim.* Whether coexistence establishes on a seed, on what fraction of seeds, or a heterotroph guild nucleates on the pile, is genesis search's to report — the same boundary that makes `C*` a characterisation makes `Λ` one.
- *Reduction regime only.* Where a drain hits its cap, or where the map's initial state never existed (every founder dying on its first tick under the peak-relative death threshold, endowment, and embodiment — per-body floors the biomass map has no coordinate for), the condition is silent.
- *The coefficient rows are lumpings, and two of them have been wrong.* The producer's low-density net growth is `r_P = χ_P·γ·(π_F − b_P)` with the biomass conversion `χ_P = (κ_P + (1−κ_P)·η_P·s) / (1 − (1−κ_P)·η_P·(1−s))`, `η_P = reproduction_efficiency·(1−φ_P)·(1−e^{−max(fecundity_P, 0.1)})`, `s` the offspring structure fraction — both of `κ_P`'s branches become biomass the map carries, the reproductive one after the heat of a birth (the correction of the earlier `κ_P·γ·(π_F − b_P)`, which dropped the reproductive share and called every `κ_P = 0` cell non-permanent). The consumer conversion `β = χ_C·γ·e·ĥ_C` in `I`, and `Λ` through `min(χ_C·γ·e_C, q/θ_C)`, carry the same-shaped `χ_C` on the consumer's own `κ_C`, dispersal and fecundity — the reproductive path is one `resolve_reproduction` for every trait vector — correcting the earlier `κ_C·γ·e·ĥ_C`, which read zero at `κ_C = 0` although the consumer's surplus becomes offspring. Either conversion is zero only on a degenerate corner no search box reaches (`reproduction_efficiency = 0`, a propagule share of 1, or `s = 0` at `κ = 0`).
- *Two compartments, one heterotroph.* Every producer is lumped into `P`, every heterotroph into `C_E`; trait-distribution branching, guild coexistence on the pile, and the realised drift of traits away from the seeded centroids are outside its coordinates.
- *Endogenous terms.* `ι`, `ν`, `q`, `μ_P`, and the kernel distance `d` are set by the realised economy, not by any committed parameter, exactly as the decomposer-return finding says of reach and richness. The only pure-parameter kills `Λ` yields are the degenerate corners that finding already names (`base_trophic_efficiency = 0`; no heterotroph ever in reach of a carcass). Everything else in `Λ` is a characterisation, and a gate would additionally need a "best heterotroph" bound over the trait box for `h_C`, `κ_C`, `θ_C`, `b_C`.

**Where the individual-based model contradicts it — the failure cells.** The condition is cross-checked against the committed stepper — with the founder trait floor, on the atlas regenerated on that stepper — on every one of its 82 live cells and 200 low-discrepancy points of the search box, 8 seeds each, at the settled horizon `T = 2000`, with the predictions evaluated from the config alone at the cluster centroids genesis seeds and `β` carrying its `χ_C` (#482). The observed verdict is the evaluator's full-series classification at `T` (the rollout stops only on extinction and explosion; a dead-pool stop is not taken as the verdict), so a cell is judged on its settled community, not its bloom. Runs stopped by the 300 s wall-clock guard (70 of 2256, on 16 dense configs) are neither collapse nor persistence: a cell's ensemble is read over its finished seeds, and three cells with no finished seed (`atlas:59`, `sample:20`, `sample:129`) are unobserved. The rule for promotion is *zero false positives*: a single config the reduction calls permanent that collapses on every seed disqualifies the predicate as a gate. The rule is scoped to configs that enter the reduction's regime: a config whose founder cohort does not survive its first tick never realises the map's initial state, so the *reduction regime only* clause makes the condition silent there, and such a **regime exit** is recorded beside the predicate but is not counted against it (#517). Applied per predicate:

- **`ρ > 1` (the sharpened extinction gate): uncontradicted, unexercised, not wired.** No cell fails it and none contradicts it, but `ρ ≥ 1.9` throughout the box, so it would exclude nothing the standing `π_F ≤ 1` gate does not; and its centroid form is not a config-only kill — `b_P` is read at the pure-producer centroid, and a founder drawn or a mutant evolved with less autotrophy pays less maintenance — so the sound config-only version *is* the standing gate.
- **`I > 1`: one strict false positive on 56 predicted-permanent cells, plus one regime exit — the positive direction fails the rule at the settled horizon.** At the 500-tick horizon it had none. **`sample:12`** (`ρ = 11.2`, `I = 2.19`; 31 founders) is extinct on 8/8 seeds and is the regime exit: every founder dies on tick 1 on seven seeds — the peak-relative death threshold, endowment and embodiment per-body floor the biomass map has no coordinate for — and on the eighth the one founder left after tick 1 dies at tick 573. **`sample:31`** (`ρ = 86`, `I = 1.40`, `Λ = 2674`, `κ_C = 0.01`; 31 founders, peak population 111) is nutrient-lockup on 8/8 seeds at `T`, a bloom-then-lockup cell: lockup is outside the two-compartment map's coordinates, and `I` sits close to 1. `sample:31` is the strict failure, and it is not argued away: the violation stands as recorded ([`509-viability-at-settled-horizon.md`](../research/509-viability-at-settled-horizon.md); read by the designer in #517). The negative direction, which is the one a prefilter runs, fails as before: see below.
- **`Λ > 1` at the reference lumping: five strict failures on 194 predicted-permanent cells (was two), plus the regime exit `sample:12`.** The five are nutrient-lockup at `T` with `ι = 1` (every carcass within every consumer's reach): the two named at the 500-tick horizon, **`sample:154`** (`Λ = 128`; only one of its eight seeds finished inside the guard, and it locks up) and **`sample:192`** (`Λ = 99`, 10 founders, peak 411, 8/8), and three more the settled horizon adds — **`atlas:2`** (a live cell; `Λ = 1166`, `I = 0.90`, 37 founders, peak 53, 8/8), **`sample:31`** (above) and **`sample:158`** (`Λ = 20`, `I = 0.005`, 44 founders, peak 466; 6/8 lockup, one extinction, one energy death). `sample:12` (above, `Λ = 7960`) is extinct, not locked up, and is out of scope as a regime exit. `ρ ∧ I` agrees with every collapse but `sample:31`, which both predicates miss. `sample:147`, the class's first-named cell, has two finished seeds at `T`, both monoculture. *Fault: the endogenous reach `ι`* — the well-mixed reading over-serves the pile, precisely the contingency the authority boundary names, and the empirical twin of the example10 reading on the nutrient side; the settled horizon exposes it on more cells because a bloom that silts its nutrient does so after tick 500 (36 of the 108 mixed cells under a permanent `Λ` are modal-lockup).

Two further gaps qualify the *negative* direction, which is the direction a gate would use.

- **`I ≤ 1` kills nothing.** Of 188 observed configs the two-compartment map calls not-permanent — 55 of the 82 atlas live cells among them — 183 persist on at least one seed (77 on all finished seeds), and 161 persist with consumers. The consumer that cannot invade the standing crop (`I` has median 0.35 over the box, because the producer↔consumer trait distance is large against `trophic_distance_decay`) persists on the **carcass pile** — the route `Λ` describes (median 763 at the reference lumping) and the two-compartment map lacks. `I > 1` characterises whether the *living-prey* route is open — a useful descriptor of an atlas cell — and is not the system's boundary; on the coupled reduction `Λ` replaces it. The evaluator's decomposer-guild observable (a sustained, recruiting population draining carcasses) is seen on only 7 of those 161 cells at `T`, against 172 of 180 at the 500-tick horizon on the earlier cross-check's guild read — the pile route's persistence is visible in the roster, its guild signature is not, and which of the two moved is not settled by this sweep.

On the earlier cross-check every one was called not-permanent by `I` and by `Λ` alike because the then-current `β = κ_C·γ·e·ĥ_C` read zero there. With `β = χ_C·γ·e·ĥ_C` the re-read is: `Λ` calls all 19 permanent (none collapses on every seed; 5 persist on all, 14 are mixed) and `I` calls 17 of them not-permanent on the merits of the trait distance, not the coefficient — the same negative-direction gap as the rest of the box.

A search-box gate on the pile route becomes possible only through the reserve remedy — a reach bound `ῑ` and a carcass floor `ν̲`; `atlas:2`, `sample:31`, `sample:154`, `sample:158` and `sample:192` are the counter-examples that would falsify any `Λ` gate drawn without them.

## Resolved finding — generalist dominance has no static gate (by design)

[Expected properties](expected-properties.md) once treated generalist dominance as prevented by superlinear *per-trait* maintenance ("a mathematical property of convex maintenance costs"). On analysis this does not hold: with *independent additive* convex costs, a generalist exploiting two niches at `(t, t)` pays `2·c·t^p` and earns roughly `2·R(t)` — exactly twice a specialist's *net* — and against a specialist at `2t` it even pays *less* (`2·c·t^p < 2^p·c·t^p` for `p > 1`). Per-trait convexity penalises concentration, not breadth; its real role is to make each trait self-limiting. (The implementation does apply this convex per-trait cost — `maintenance_cost_exponent`, default 2 — so the *form* is real and committed; it was the *attribution*, not the cost, that was wrong.)

The system design has since been corrected ([world rules](world-rules.md), cost structure; [expected properties](expected-properties.md), generalist dominance; [trait space](trait-space.md), "No L1 budget constraint"). The anti-generalist force is the conjunction of **structural fragility** (cost-structure trade-off #9 — a broad, high-entropy body crosses its peak-relative death threshold after a smaller structural loss, raising both predation and starvation mortality) and **functional incompatibility** (trade-off #2 — the substrate-anchored stillness autotrophy and nutrient uptake require is broken by mobility, so the broadest generalist cannot physically express its traits). Both are committed mechanisms; neither is a static income/cost inequality. Fragility acts through mortality and incompatibility through morphology — so generalist-dominance prevention is a **dynamics** property (stability of the differentiated state), not an existence gate decidable in closed form. By design there is no clean static gate; this failure mode sits with monoculture and frozen dynamics, beyond cheap a priori reach for now.

A static gate *would* become available if the design committed a **cross-trait interaction maintenance term** (a cost in the product of co-invested traits): a generalist would then be dominated whenever its interaction overhead plus its fragility-driven mortality exceeded its multi-niche income gain — a closed-form inequality. The design holds this term in reserve rather than committing it (it adds a knob and is unnecessary if fragility and incompatibility suffice), to be promoted only if genesis search finds generalists dominating where they should be confined — the same contingency the world rules already name for autotrophy–mobility. Until then the prevention is real but dynamic, and the matching gate stays out of reach.

This is recorded as the first product of the lens: an a priori check that caught a place where the stated mechanism did not produce the expected property, and drove a system-design correction rather than a viability one.

**Empirical confirmation — the prediction holds (#325).** The above was, until tested, a *prediction*: that fragility + incompatibility confine generalists without the interaction term. The example lens now confirms it. `scenarios/example12_generalist_dominance.json` seeds four archetypes in one viable, differentiated world (`clustering_strength` 1.0, `trophic_balance_score` 1.0, survives 2000 ticks on all 8 seeds): specialist producers, specialist mobile consumers, **broad** generalists (autotrophy + heterotrophy + mobility), and **compatible** sessile mixotrophs (autotrophy + heterotrophy) — the broad and compatible pair seeded identically but for mobility (0.5 vs 0.0), an in-run control on the sessile/mobile incompatibility axis. Reading the energy-weighted breadth measure off survivor traits (`probe_generalist`), with `mobility_maintenance_cost = 0`, `wear_rate = 0`, and **no cross-trait interaction term committed**:

- **broad generalists are eliminated** — 0 survivors, 0.0% energy share on every seed, despite an energy advantage and co-location with both light and prey. This is the confined class: the mobile mixotroph (the rooted-producer + roving-hunter the autotrophy–mobility incompatibility rules out);
- **compatible (sessile) mixotrophs persist and now rise to rough parity** — 33–56% of energy (median 44%), at or just under the 50% line and tipping past it on one seed (seed 4: 56%). They are a legal, *non*-incompatible combination (autotrophy + heterotrophy, no mobility), so the design never predicted them confined; fragility alone holds them off runaway, and it does — they co-exist with specialists rather than displacing them;
- specialists hold the majority on most seeds (and the specialist *mobile* consumers, like the broad generalists, are out-competed to 0 here — a competitive loss in this economy, not a feeding failure: see the un-confounding note below); the evaluator's own `generalist_dominance` gate reads 0/8.

So the committed structural fragility (#9) and functional incompatibility (#2) suffice on their own: the differentiated state is stable and the broadest (incompatible) generalist cannot establish. **The interaction term stays in reserve** — the contingency that would promote it (genesis search finding generalists dominating *where they should be confined*) has not fired: the confined class is empty, and the compatible mixotroph that rose to parity is not in that class. The one thing to watch is that the compatible mixotroph's energy share roughly doubled once mobile feeding worked (median 20% → 44%); it sits near parity, not runaway, but if a future sweep shows it crossing decisively into dominance, fragility-alone would be the term under pressure. See [`scenarios/verdicts.md`](../../scenarios/verdicts.md) for the per-seed read.

**Re-validated — the confound is resolved; the prediction holds un-confounded (#380, fixes #379).** The numbers above are from runs **after** the fix for #379. The earlier confirmation had been read from runs in which a contact-duration ramp on consumption (since removed in #380; see [world rules](world-rules.md#consumption-is-incremental-and-its-character-emerges-from-target-and-size)) reset on every movement and so left *any* mobile consumer unable to feed at all — confounding `example12`'s in-run control, since the broad generalists and specialist mobile consumers are mobile (0.5 and 0.3) while the surviving compatible mixotrophs are sessile (0.0). With consumption now a binary-reach drain, mobile consumers demonstrably feed: a dedicated integration test asserts a moving consumer co-located with prey draws real predation energy and survives past tick 2. (The predator–prey `example10_predator_prey_hopf` is *not* such evidence — a per-tick trace (#400) shows its low-mobility consumers fail to establish on the sparse, trait-distant standing crop and go extinct by tick 2, leaving a producer monoculture; its earlier-claimed "signature Hopf oscillation, median 0.36" was finite-N producer-reproduction pulsing, not a trophic cycle, and the mean-field Hopf prediction is recorded as falsified in [`F-hopf-validation.md`](../research/F-hopf-validation.md).) The broad generalist is therefore eliminated *despite being able to feed* — its confinement is attributable to incompatibility (#2) plus fragility (#9), not to broken feeding. The #325 prediction (fragility + incompatibility confine generalists without an interaction term) now has clean, un-confounded support.

## Place in the validation triad

Viability is one of three complementary lenses on the quality of the system design (see [expected properties](expected-properties.md#how-we-test-for-these-properties)):

1. **A priori viability** (this document) — nearly free; rules regions out in closed form.
2. **Diagnostic examples** (`scenarios/`) — a hand-built initial condition probing a specific failure mode, run headless to observe whether it occurs or the system recovers.
3. **[Genesis search](genesis-search.md)** — the broad, expensive empirical sweep of parameter space. It does not return one tuned config; it **illuminates** the space into an *atlas* — a map onto the failure-mode coordinates with a dead frontier of where worlds fail and on which cliff. The atlas's dead frontier is the empirical realisation of this document's negative gates.

This document's gates are not merely *checked* by lens 3 — they are **wired into** it. The two committed closed-form gates (extinction's `F ≤ B`; energy death's `N_total` floor) run as a **prefilter** ahead of the genesis rollouts: a provably-dead config is routed to the atlas's dead frontier without spending an ensemble, shrinking the search box exactly as this document's [payoff](#what-this-document-is-and-is-not) promises. Because the prefilter and the rollout verdict the *same* config, every genesis sweep continuously falsifies the gates — a config gated extinct that survives a rollout localises a mis-drawn gate. The lockup cliff is deliberately *not* prefiltered: it has no cheap gate (it is decomposer-mass-dependent and emergent per seed), so the atlas maps it by running, not gating. The sustained-count ceiling is not prefiltered either, for a different reason: it excludes nothing — it is an affirmative bound every living state obeys, falsified by any run whose mean population exceeds `π_F·m²`, and so far none does. The permanence characterisation is not prefiltered for a third reason: its positive direction is contradicted by the individual-based model on named cells and its negative direction on most of the box, so it describes atlas cells (`ρ`, `I`, `Λ` at the seeded centroids) rather than routing them.

The three are bound together by the **example file**, the only shared concrete object: the *same* initial condition can be predicted by viability, observed by a simulation run, and located relative to the search. That makes the example the unit of cross-validation — a viability gate is a *falsifiable prediction*, and the cheapest place to test it is a single example run. Agreement across the lenses raises confidence; disagreement is diagnostic:

- viability says dead, the run shows life → the gate is wrong or too conservative, or models a mechanism the design lacks;
- viability says nothing, the run fails → the gate set is incomplete, or the failure is a dynamics failure beyond cheap reach.

For this loop to work, an example must carry not just its initial condition but the **failure mode it probes, viability's prediction, and the observed outcome**. Today examples carry only the initial condition; closing that gap is tracked as follow-up work.

## Findings and follow-ups

- **Promote committed forms into system design — done for trophic transfer.** The efficiency-vs-distance form (`base_trophic_efficiency · exp(−trophic_distance_decay · d)`) is now owned by [world rules](world-rules.md) (flow 7), not just parameterised in the scenario files. With the form pinned, the trophic-pyramid-height question moves from conditional to parametric: pyramid height is bounded by how many transfers can each retain enough of the level below to clear metabolism, and the per-transfer ceiling is now a closed form in `base_trophic_efficiency` and `trophic_distance_decay` rather than a free shape.
- **Nutrient lockup — reached as far as the committed fluxes allow.** The carcass-locked nutrient the energy-death gate sets aside now has a closed-form stationary fraction `C* = death_flux / decomp_turnover` (the flux-balance characterisation) and a conditional decomposer-return floor whose clean gate is blocked only by the under-committed carcass-richness/reach terms (the resolved finding). The characterisation makes `example9_detrital_pathway`'s `nutrient_lockup` verdict a falsifiable prediction — viability's first concrete prediction on an existing example, the cross-validation unit the triad turns on. A standing `C*` is itself the faithful equilibrium of the conserved stock, not a deficiency (the partial-closure finding): the criterion is `A*` above the floor with `C` bounded, and the committed body-extent reach form pins reach's shape but not its magnitude, so it does not promote `C*` to a gate (`example13_closed_web` confirms; long-horizon boundedness is genesis-search territory).
- **Population explosion — the count half is bounded, the energy half is open.** The committed energy budget proves a per-tick solar income cap `F·m²` and a sustained-count ceiling `N̄_max = π_F·m²` (the solar-ceiling bound) — the first existence-side affirmative statement, holding on every genesis run checked at the settled horizon `T = 2000`, the income cap reached exactly and the count ceiling with two orders of magnitude to spare. A ceiling on living *energy* does not follow: flow 9's reproductive allocation is untaxed, uncapped, and discharged only by an event the rules never force (the open finding). The per-unit holding cost that would close it is held in reserve alongside the interaction term and the reach bound. The evaluator's fixed population ceiling is a runtime performance guard, not a reading of either result.
- **Permanence — the reduction's first constructive result, held as a characterisation.** The mean-field reduction is permanent — its extinction and lockup boundaries repel — under a closed-form condition in the groups (`ρ > 1`, `I > 1` on the energy map; `Λ > 1` and the heteroclinic-cycle criterion on the coupled reduction), with the lockup corner shown to be uninvadable by producers at any flux. Cross-checked against the fixed stepper on the regenerated atlas at the settled horizon `T = 2000` with the corrected `β`, `ρ ∧ I` has one strict failure cell on 56 predicted-permanent configs (`sample:31`, bloom-then-lockup) and `Λ` has five on 194 (`atlas:2`, `sample:31`, `154`, `158`, `192` — the endogenous reach over-serving the pile); `sample:12`, extinct because its founders die on tick 1, is a regime exit outside the rule's scope; the negative direction of `I` is defeated by the carcass route on 183 of 188 cells; the 19 live cells at `κ_C = 0` are re-read with `χ_C` and no longer sit on the coefficient. It is wired into nothing. The strict failures of the positive direction are recorded, not argued away, and were read by the designer (#517), who declined to spend the reserve remedy on them; the remaining follow-up — a prefilter permanence gate over whatever subregion a reach bound and carcass floor would make safe — stays deferred behind those reserve commitments (#465).
- **Anti-generalist mechanism — stated, gate deferred by design.** Prevention now rests on structural fragility (#9) and functional incompatibility (#2); a closed-form generalist-dominance gate becomes possible only if a cross-trait interaction maintenance term is later committed (see the resolved finding above). Tracked as a contingency, not current work.
