# Research #462 — Do the dimensionless groups explain the search box? A π-space explanatory check

**Status: measurement on existing data. Commits nothing in the stepper, evaluator or search;
re-scopes #462.** The genesis search runs in 32 raw dimensions, and #462 (filed by
[440](440-dimensionless-groups.md)) proposed re-parameterising it in the dimensionless groups of
[viability](../system-design/viability.md) on the argument that the physics reads only ratios. Before
rewriting `decode`, this note asks the cheaper empirical question: **over the search box, how much
of a config's outcome do the groups the theory already computes explain, against the raw
coordinates?** The data are the #509 permanence sweep (`target/permanence-crosscheck.jsonl`, SHA-1
`e92eb3709796251ea6e3c0dd6d506b777b16b731`: 82 atlas live cells + the seed-421 LHS draw of 200
points, 8 seeds each at `T = 2000`), which records `ρ`, `I`, `Λ`, `κ_C` and the twelve other A1/A2
scalars per config beside every seed's terminal mode. Stepper and search at `c68b467`.

## TL;DR

1. **The permanence groups explain ~5 % of outcome variance over the box; the raw coordinates
   explain ~35–40 %.** 10-fold cross-validated `R²` for a config's live fraction on the unbiased LHS
   sample (n = 198): `ρ, I, Λ, κ_C` **0.07**; all sixteen A1/A2 scalars **0.04**; the 32 raw unit
   coordinates **0.38**. Lockup fraction: 0.05 / 0.05 / **0.34**. Adding the groups to the raw
   coordinates changes nothing (0.37). The pooled set (atlas + sample, n = 279) reads the same.
2. **The signal is one raw dimension the permanence theory does not read:
   `light_competition_radius`.** It is the strongest univariate predictor of both live (Spearman
   **+0.52**) and lockup (**−0.42**), and the largest ridge coefficient for both, twice the next.
   Binned by quintile of its searched range the effect is monotone and large: live fraction
   0.36 → 0.91, lockup 0.39 → 0.01, median peak population 83 → 29 from the smallest to the largest
   radius. Small radius, weak light competition, tall producer bloom, bloom-then-lockup.
3. **The theory does hold the missing coordinate — in the count ceiling, not in permanence.** The
   sustained-count ceiling `N̄_max = π_F·m²` carries `m = ⌊√2/π_r⌋ + 1` with `π_r = r/L`.
   `log m²` alone reads Spearman **+0.47** with lockup (better than bare `r`) and −0.40 with live;
   `ρ, I, Λ, κ_C` **plus `log m²`** reaches CV `R²` **0.33** for live and 0.22 for lockup — most of
   the way to the raw coordinates' 0.38 / 0.34 from five numbers. The permanence reduction is
   blind to the bloom amplitude the ceiling bounds.
4. **The effective dimension is small, but it is not the π-groups as listed.** The six raw dims
   with the largest ridge weights (`light_competition_radius`, `mean_mobility`, `mean_kappa`,
   `world_extent`, `contact_range_coefficient`, `solar_flux_magnitude`) alone reach CV `R²` 0.43
   for live — *better* than all 32, so the other 26 are noise at this budget. `π_r = r/L` as #440
   tabulates it reads +0.40 with live, worse than bare `r` (+0.52); `L` alone reads +0.08.
5. **Nothing explains the decomposer guild, because there is nothing to explain**: 16 guild-positive
   seeds in 2186 finished (mean per-config fraction 0.007). That is #519's question, not this one's.

**Consequence for #462.** Re-parameterising the search in the 29 tabulated groups would put the
search's covariance adaptation on coordinates that carry ~5 % of the outcome signal and would
express the one dominant coordinate in a form (`r/L`) that predicts *worse* than the raw field.
#462 is re-scoped: the reduced coordinates are to be derived from data of this kind (the count
ceiling's `m²` is the first candidate the theory itself supplies), not taken from the permanence
table; see the issue.

Pooled (n = 279): live 0.07 / 0.06 / **0.37**; lockup 0.10 / 0.10 / **0.40**; consumers
0.04 / 0.05 / **0.25** (linear, same column order). The same ordering, the same gap.

Three things to read off the table. The groups sit at the noise floor on every outcome. The raw
coordinates carry a real linear signal on live, lockup and peak population. And the quadratic and
kNN fits are *worse* than linear in every set — at n ≈ 200 with 8 seeds per config there is not
enough data to see interactions, so the linear reading is the one this sweep can support, and the
unexplained 60 % is seed noise plus whatever is non-linear, not evidence of no further structure.

## 4. `light_competition_radius`, binned

LHS sample, quintiles of the unit coordinate (the searched range is `1 ≤ r ≤ 20`; `world_extent`
is searched independently over 20–100):

| `light_competition_radius` (unit) | n | live | lockup | consumers | median peak pop |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.00–0.21 | 40 | 0.36 | 0.39 | 0.25 | 83 |
| 0.21–0.41 | 39 | 0.63 | 0.27 | 0.40 | 41 |
| 0.41–0.60 | 40 | 0.74 | 0.16 | 0.38 | 32 |
| 0.60–0.80 | 39 | 0.87 | 0.03 | 0.38 | 31 |
| 0.80–1.00 | 40 | 0.91 | 0.01 | 0.35 | 29 |

The mechanism is the one the count ceiling states: the radius sets how many producers can each
take the full flux — `m = ⌊√2·L/r⌋ + 1` — so a small radius lifts the bloom's ceiling, the bloom
peaks higher (83 vs 29), the death flux off the peak silts the nutrient into carcasses, and the
world is on the lockup cliff by `T`. The consumer fraction is flat across the bins: the radius
decides whether the world *survives its own bloom*, not whether it holds a second trophic level.

## 5. The ceiling's geometric factor as the missing group

Computed from the decoded `world_extent` and `light_competition_radius` per config:

| outcome | Spearman vs `log m²` | vs raw `r` | vs `π_r = r/L` |
|---|---|---|---|
| live | −0.40 | +0.52 | +0.40 |
| lockup | **+0.47** | −0.42 | — |
| log peak population | +0.34 | −0.32 | — |
| consumers | +0.10 | +0.12 | — |

Linear CV `R²`: `log m²` alone — live 0.23, lockup 0.14; **π4 + `log m²`** — live **0.33**,
lockup **0.22**. Five numbers recover most of what the 32 raw coordinates give (0.38 / 0.34). This
is the reading that matters for #462: the theory's *own* count ceiling supplies the coordinate the
permanence groups lack, and a reduced coordinate set built from what the box actually shows
(`m²`, the bloom amplitude; then the mobility / kappa / contact-range directions of §3, which
have no group yet) is a different object from the 29-row table in [440](440-dimensionless-groups.md).

Why bare `r` still beats `r/L` and `log m²` on *live*: the ceiling is a ceiling, not a bloom
predictor — `m²` bounds the sustained count while the bloom's height depends on the founder
budget and the solar flux too — and the box searches `L` over a narrower relative range than `r`,
so `r` alone carries most of the ratio's variance. The coordinate is real; its exact form is for
the re-scoped issue to derive, not this note.

## 6. What this does not settle

- **Interactions.** Every non-linear fit is worse than linear here. Whether the six dims of §3
  interact (a radius that only matters at high solar flux, say) needs either more configs or a
  targeted two-factor sweep; this sweep cannot see it.
- **The guild.** 16 positive seeds in 2186 is too few to regress on; #519 must first decide
  whether the read or the community moved.
- **Causation.** The binned table is observational over an LHS draw with all 32 dims varying;
  the radius's effect is not confounded by construction (LHS), but a single-factor sweep at a fixed
  live baseline is the clean confirmation and costs ~40 rollouts.
- **The atlas population.** The atlas cells are selected, and their outcomes at `T = 2000` include
  the 213 / 656 bloom-then-lockup runs #505 found; nothing here re-reads them separately.
